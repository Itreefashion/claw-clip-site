Hi there,

Luquan Gewu Zhixiang E-commerce Company is a manufacturer and wholesaler specializing in high-end hair clips with a mature and elegant aesthetic. Our hair clips are crafted via injection molding, using premium PC and TPEE materials, available in a wide variety of shapes and designs.

We take great care in every production step: from raw material selection, injection molding, initial inspection and sorting, spring assembly, spray painting, final quality check to packaging. Rigorous control across all procedures guarantees superior quality for every piece. Customers can instantly feel the outstanding quality of our hair clips and recognize they offer excellent value for money.

We also provide custom services tailored to your requirements. Focused exclusively on high-end hair clip solutions, we welcome your inquiries and collaborations.
