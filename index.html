Hi there,

Luquan Gewu Zhixiang E-commerce Company is a professional manufacturer and wholesaler of premium hair claws, built around a mature, elegant aesthetic. Our hair claws are crafted from high quality PC, TPEE and natural cellulose acetate materials via injection molding or sheet processing, available in a wide range of shapes and designs.

We exercise rigorous care at every production stage: raw material selection, processing, preliminary inspection, spring assembly, painting, final quality testing and packaging. Strict oversight across all procedures ensures outstanding quality for every piece. Customers can instantly feel the superior craftsmanship of our hair claws and recognize their excellent value.

We also offer custom services tailored to your brand requirements. Focused on premium hair claw solutions, we welcome your inquiries and cooperation.

PC Hair Claw

Crafted from premium polycarbonate for a smooth, durable finish. PC offers excellent strength and a refined surface that keeps its shape season after season.

Cellulose Acetate Hair Claw

TPEE Hair Claw

Our Collections

Ben is the visionary behind our brand.

Driven by a passion for refined, timeless hair accessories, Ben identified a market demand for quiet luxury hair claws: pieces that carry the sophisticated aesthetic of acetate clips, yet deliver dependable functionality. Resistant to breakage and deformation, whether for styling, photoshoots or everyday wear, they unite beauty with lasting performance. With this vision, he founded our brand, dedicated to premium hair claws.

Backed by profound expertise in manufacturing craftsmanship and a refined aesthetic sensibility, Ben established uncompromising quality standards. He advocates meticulous inspections throughout the entire production journey — from the selection of premium raw materials to finished goods and packaging — ensuring every product reaches our customers in impeccable condition.

Upholding Ben’s pursuit of excellence, our team offers customized, serialized production tailored to each client’s brand positioning, helping our partners build their distinct brand identity. We craft elegant, reliable hair accessories that bring quiet confidence to every wearer.
